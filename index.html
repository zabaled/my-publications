Localizing failures in AI agent pipelines with structured execution traces

Alexey Zabegin

American Impact Review | Published April 30, 2026

DOI: 10.66308/air.e69362342

Read Publication View Article & PDF DOI Link

Abstract

This research examines failure localization in AI agent pipelines using structured execution traces.

The study evaluates whether preserving execution-trace field identities improves the ability to identify failure locations compared with flattened textual representations.

The evaluation includes 219 failed executions and 5,936 candidate steps across three AI agent systems.

The findings indicate that structured execution traces can support lightweight diagnostic triage, while the benefit of separate-field encoding for exact failure localization remains uncertain.
